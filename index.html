A proposed human-supervised research and planning assistant for an independent digital-download business.

This website describes the project. It is not a live marketplace analytics service, and Etsy API access and authorization for the proposed research have not yet been obtained.

Application purpose

The application is intended for the owner's personal business use. Planned capabilities include evaluating digital product opportunities, comparing permitted marketplace information, identifying customer needs, and planning original digital products. Human review will govern creative and business decisions.

Proposed use of Etsy data

Subject to Etsy's express written authorization and approved access, the application would use public listing information such as titles, categories, tags, prices, descriptions and public shop information for private, AI-assisted market research and product planning.

  • No unauthorized automated collection or analysis.
  • No public redistribution, resale or AI model training using Etsy data.
  • No access to private competitor information or automatic listing publication.
  • Any processing by third-party AI services would require applicable authorization and appropriate data safeguards.

Research remains disabled until the relevant permissions are confirmed. See Etsy's API Terms of Use.

Privacy and data handling

Current website: This static informational site has no account registration, forms, analytics scripts or application data collection. Its hosting provider, GitHub, may process ordinary technical access information under its own privacy statement.

Planned application: The intended scope is a single owner, with only necessary and authorized data processed. API credentials would be kept out of public files. Any retained marketplace data would be access-restricted, minimized, and deleted according to applicable Etsy requirements. No buyer personal data is intended to be collected for market research. Before live use, the operator will confirm the actual processors, retention and deletion procedures and update this notice accordingly.

This notice describes the current development status and planned safeguards, not a claim that the proposed integration is operational.
